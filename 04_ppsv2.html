Instrumen

Use the official PPSv2 chart and instructions. Because PPSv2 scoring is a horizontal “best fit” assessment with leftward-column precedence, this application intentionally does not invent an independent algorithm from isolated dropdown domains.

Hasil

Isi seluruh data yang diperlukan lalu pilih Hitung & Interpretasikan.

PPSv2 is an official copyrighted tool. The app preserves the official level structure and requires the clinician to determine the best-fit PPSv2 level using the official chart/instructions; it does not silently modify the scoring method.
